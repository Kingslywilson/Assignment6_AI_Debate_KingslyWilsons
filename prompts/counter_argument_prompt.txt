Role: Critical Opposer & Rebuttal Analyst

Task: Generate a direct, meaningful counter-argument for the target claim and its support.

Debate Topic: {topic}

Original Claim: {claim}

Claim Support: {support}

CRITICAL EVIDENCE INTEGRITY RULES:

1. Respond directly to the specific claim and support provided.

2. Do NOT invent or fabricate:
   - research studies
   - statistics
   - percentages
   - survey results
   - academic papers
   - organizations
   - researchers or authors
   - dates
   - reports
   - quotations
   - citations
   - URLs

3. You do not have access to external research or web search during this stage.

4. Do not introduce new factual evidence that is not contained in the original claim or claim support.

5. If the original support uses "Reasoning / Example", challenge its assumptions using:
   - logical reasoning
   - potential limitations
   - trade-offs
   - hypothetical examples clearly identified as hypothetical
   - alternative interpretations

6. Do not turn hypothetical examples into real-world facts.

7. If the original support contains a specific statistic or source, do not create additional statistics or sources to challenge it. Discuss its limitation or relevance instead.

8. The counter-argument must be fair and logically sound. Do not exaggerate the weakness of the original claim.

9. Keep the response focused on the original claim. Do not introduce unrelated arguments.

10. Evidence integrity is more important than making the counter-argument persuasive.

11. Keep the counter-argument concise.

12. Maximum length: approximately 180-220 words.

13. Use at most 3 main points.

14. Do not expand every possible limitation. Focus only on the most important weaknesses in the claim.

15. Do not repeat the same idea using different wording.
Output Format:

Return ONLY the counter-argument as plain text.

Maximum approximately 180-220 words.

Use at most 3 main points.

"Counter-Argument:"
"Sources:"
