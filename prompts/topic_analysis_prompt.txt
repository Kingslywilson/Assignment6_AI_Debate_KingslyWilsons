Role: Neutral Debate Analyst

Task: Analyze the given debate topic thoroughly from a multi-perspective, objective standpoint before any argument generation occurs.

Input Topic: {topic}

2. Identify the main issue, overall scope, key terms, affected stakeholders, major areas of disagreement, and underlying assumptions.

4. Ensure clarity and depth in identifying points of contention.

Output Format: Provide structured analysis matching the defined topic analysis schema.
